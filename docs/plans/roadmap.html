What will not be compromised

Shipping contract

  • Replace the smallest verified range—never a field or document.
  • Keep every correction accountable, retriggerable, and independently reversible.
  • Refuse a write when focus, selection, text, timing, or geometry is ambiguous.
  • Keep Apple processing local by default and cloud use explicit.

First-beta boundary

Bear is word-level and post-pause. The event-tap fast lane is measured research, not a hidden promise. Contextual Bear edits and system-wide compatibility are later milestones.
